04 · About me

I'm a game designer who lets data guide design. For 3.5 years, I worked as a Product Specialist at Metabora (King Studio) on Friends Town and Friends Popcorn. I studied how players spent, saved, and got stuck, then turned those insights into changes to stages, the economy, and events.

I'm studying Computer Game Design at George Mason University, where I serve as Student Representative (President) of the Department of Game Design and received the Student of the Year Award in 2022. There, I led and designed Adventure Match! and The Witch's House, and created two campaign games, Covid Jump and City Plogger.

What drives me is making games measurably better for the people who play them. Outside of work, you'll find me hiking, canoeing, or barbecuing at a campsite.
